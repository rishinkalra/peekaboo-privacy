Peekaboo: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Peekaboo is a play app for young children. It is designed to collect nothing.

We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases, and it never sends anything over the internet.

Photos, camera and microphone. In the grown-ups area ("Faces & voices"), a parent can take or choose family photos and record short voice clips. These are stored only inside the app on your device. They are never uploaded or shared, and they are deleted when you remove them in the app or delete the app. The camera, photo library and microphone are used only when a parent chooses to add a photo or record a clip.

Settings. Play time, volume, music, vibration, places and which sets appear behind the doors are saved on your device only.

Children. Because no personal information is collected, nothing is collected from children. The app complies with the Apple App Store Kids Category and Google Play Families policies.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
